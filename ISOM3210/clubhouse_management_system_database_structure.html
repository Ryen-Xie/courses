Reservation Table Child / Junction Table (N)

Stores booking records and links Members, Facilities, and Staff (Administrator check-ins/check-outs) using Foreign Keys.

reservation_id (PK) date timeslot status special_request member_id (FK) facility_id (FK) processed_by_staff_id (FK)
5001 2026-04-20 10:00 - 11:00 Arrived Turn off AC 101 1 201
5002 2026-04-20 14:00 - 16:00 Confirmed None 102 2 NULL

Key Teaching Points for Foreign Keys:

  • Linking Staff to Actions: In Reservation #5001, the foreign key processed_by_staff_id = 201 points to Alex Chan in the Staff table. This reflects the check-in process handled by an Administrator (UC-004 & UC-005).
  • Nullable Foreign Keys: Note that Reservation #5002 has a NULL value for processed_by_staff_id. This demonstrates that a member can reserve a facility online (UC-002) before any staff member processes their check-in at the clubhouse.
  • Entity Relationships: The Reservation table links three independent parent entities: Member (Who booked), Facility (What was booked), and Staff (Who checked them in).
